# FrameNova support

**English** | [繁體中文](SUPPORT.zh_TW.md)

FrameNova is a multi-track video editor for Android, iPhone, iPad and Mac. This page explains how to get help with the
app.

## Contact

Open an [issue](https://github.com/daocha/FrameNova/issues) to ask a question, report a bug or suggest an
improvement. Issues need a free GitHub account and are public, so leave out personal information and private media.

You can also email [ray@dcha.xyz](mailto:ray@dcha.xyz), for example if you have no GitHub account or your question is
private.

## What to include

- The app version, shown in **Settings**, and where you installed it from: Google Play, the App Store, the Mac App
  Store or GitHub.
- Your device model and its Android, iOS, iPadOS or macOS version.
- What you did, what you expected and what happened instead.
- For a problem with a clip or an export: the clip's resolution, frame rate and format, and the export settings you
  chose.

## Logs

To include logs, tap the version number in **Settings** ten times to unlock the developer tools. **Developer logs**
shows recent app logs, media-library warnings and errors, and the last crash, with options to copy, share or save
them. On Android, saved logs go to `Download/FrameNova/logs/`.

Logs can include the names of your files. Look through them before you attach them to a public issue. Nothing leaves
your device unless you share it. The tools lock again after the next update or when you turn them off.

## Common questions

**Where are my exports?** On Android, in `Movies/FrameNova`. On iPhone, iPad and Mac, share the export or save it to
your photo library when it finishes.

**An export stopped on my iPhone, iPad or Mac.** Keep FrameNova open during long exports. Android can finish exports
in the background, with a notification.

**Moving or trimming a clip left a gap.** This is intended: the rest of your timeline stays where it is. Delete the
gap when you want to close it.

**A Traditional Chinese font won't download.** These fonts download once, when you ask for them, and need an internet
connection. Check your connection and try again; after that, they stay on your device.

**Where are my projects?** Only on your device. Removing the app removes its projects, so save the exports you want
to keep first.

**How do I update the app?** The Google Play, App Store and Mac App Store versions update through their stores. The
GitHub version checks for new releases and offers them in the app.

**How do I remove my referral records?** Open an issue with your referral code, shown in **Settings**. Removal can
take away restored unlocks and referral credit.

## Privacy

Your projects and media are processed on your device and are not sent to the developer. Read the full
[privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
